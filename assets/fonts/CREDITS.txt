AncizarSerif-Regular.ttf  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Ancizar+Serif  (display type, as in the Figma file)
AncizarSerif-Italic.ttf   https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Ancizar+Serif  (static weight-400 instances of the variable font)
Jersey15-Regular.ttf      https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Jersey+15      (body text, 15px)
Jersey20-Regular.ttf      https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Jersey+20      (numbers, 20px)
Jersey25-Regular.ttf      https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Jersey+25      (big numbers, 25px)
Jersey10-Regular.ttf      https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Jersey+10      (small text on cards and chips, 10px)

The Jersey cuts are drawn at their native pixel sizes. Ancizar Serif is only drawn into baked
surfaces that go through the same dither as the Figma title cards.

The logo, mascot and shadow-creature artwork (assets/img) comes from the project's Figma file.
logo.png is the two-tone "Draft" lockup from the Figma title frame; mascot-silhouette.png is the
mascot's alpha mask, used for the chromatic smear on title cards.
